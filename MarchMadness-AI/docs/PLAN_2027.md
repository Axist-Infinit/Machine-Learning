# Plan: MarchMadness-AI → production-grade predictions for March 2027

## Context

In March 2027 you'll rely on this for four things:
- calibrated win % and bracket odds
- a pool entry
- bets on common bet types (moneyline, spread, total, team totals, futures) at any sportsbook
- a Kaggle March Mania entry covering men's and women's

The bar you set is **the most accurate score predictions possible**. Every bet type is priced from the score.

**Why today's system can't carry that (verified this session):**
- **The score side is broken by a market-line bug.**
  - `src/mm26_xgb/modeling.py:340` sets `MarginBase = MarketSpreadTeamA`. The market's expected margin is −spread, so the sign is wrong.
  - `:575-577` and `:736-738` then add the line back onto models that learned full scores, because fewer than 1% of training rows had lines.
  - 2025 tournament backtest (67 games), vs the market:
    - Predicted totals ran +20 high. First Four games came out 105–105, the per-team cap.
    - Margins collapsed to about 0; the model took the underdog against the spread 84% of the time.
    - The total uncertainty estimate was σ = 67 against a realized 23.
    - Against the spread 43%, over/under 48%.
  - Live March 2026 runs still ran +8.8 high on totals.
  - Separately, `modeling.py:561` computes the margin–total correlation on mirrored rows, which makes it 0 by construction.
- **No evidence of skill.**
  - The Brier score was 0.139 vs the market's 0.147, but 2025 was chalky: favorites won 79% against 71.5% implied.
  - Sharpening the market's own probabilities ×1.5 scores 0.140.
- **Training leaks outcomes.**
  - Snapshot rows (`run_march_madness.py` [RMM] :2951-3231) carry each game's own result through end-of-window aggregates, rankings and seeds.
  - `CurrTourney*` features encode the target.
  - Lines merge onto the wrong meeting of the same two teams (`modeling.py:150-152`).
  - "Closing" lines can come from after tip-off (RMM:718-720).
  - The undated 2026 external CSVs get stamped onto every season.
- **The code that runs is fragile.**
  - The 4,432-line monolith is what runs; the bundled model is never loaded.
  - The bracket is a single favorites-advance pass and doesn't lock completed games.
  - Bets are never sized; the betting flags do nothing.
  - Unresolved team names are dropped silently (2026: Miami FL and Queens results lost).
  - The only in-season results feed is the Odds API's 3-day `/scores` endpoint.
  - Men's only, no Kaggle writer, 2026 hard-coded.
- **The 2027 format changes.**
  - Men's and women's both go to **76 teams**: 12 Opening Round games, 52 teams straight to the Round of 64.
  - Men's Opening Round: Mar 16–17 (Dayton and Wichita). Women's: Mar 17–18 on the campuses of top-16 seeds.
  - Selection Sunday is **Mar 14** (DayNum 132; Kaggle's DayZero = 2026-11-02, matching 2026's 2025-11-03 pattern). Final Four Apr 3/5 in Detroit.
- **Compliance issue.** The GitHub repo is **public**, and it tracks the Kaggle competition zip (36MB) plus files derived from it. Kaggle's rules generally prohibit redistributing competition data.

**Evidence for the direction (quick checks this session).** A roughly 30-line ridge offense/defense ratings model, using only pre-tournament scores and no ML:

| On the same 67 games | Margin MAE | Total MAE | Win Brier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ridge ratings | **8.30** | **11.02** | 0.141 |
| Closing market | 8.43 | 10.83 | 0.147 |
| Current model | 11.55 | 25.07 | 0.139 |

- **Across 13 tournaments (2012–25):** margin MAE 9.20, total MAE 13.19, Brier 0.190 vs 0.198 for the seed benchmark (better in 10 of 13 seasons). **That is the floor to beat.** Totals are the weak spot.
- **Recency weighting:** a 30-day half-life made margins much worse (12.19), so recency weighting has to be tuned.
- **Free historical lines:** ESPN's per-event odds give multi-book lines for men's games back to 2017, with open/close fields from 2025.
  - On the 2025 tournament they match the cached Odds API closing consensus on 67/67 games: spread MAE 0.64 (92% within 1 pt), total MAE 1.16.
  - Equally accurate against actual results: total MAE 10.64 vs 10.62.
  - Whether pre-2025 entries are true closing lines still has to be verified.
- **Evaluation pool:** about 5,900 men's postseason games in 2012–26 (3,800 in 2017–26, the years with lines), and similar for women's.

**Outcome:**
- One season-agnostic package with a possession-based ratings engine at its core.
- It is blended with market-implied ratings and validated walk-forward against pre-registered gates.
- It produces one score distribution for every game, from which the bet probabilities, a 76-team Monte Carlo bracket, a pool entry, a Kaggle submission and a betting card are all derived.
- Staking switches on only if the betting gate passes. **The expected, honest outcome is fair lines only.**

## Design

**The core is the ratings engine, not a GBM.** Market-implied ratings are the biggest accuracy lever.

```
sources: Kaggle M/W · ESPN results+box scores+historical lines · Odds API live
   → ids/ crosswalk (strict) → features/ as-of (strictly pre-tip)
   → model/ ratings + dispersion → market/ blend → one score distribution per game
   → products/: bet probabilities · 76-team bracket MC · pool · Kaggle · card/ledger/CLV
eval/: walk-forward + prereg.yaml gates wrap all of it
```

**Layout (`src/mmai/`, on branch `mmai-2027`):** the old code is frozen in `legacy/`, excluded from CI, and deleted after M5.
- `config.py`: dates derived from the season
- `ids/`: crosswalk, resolver, `aliases.csv`
- `sources/`: kaggle, espn, odds_api, odds_logger, pick_rates
- `store/`: parquet files, hashes, append-only prediction archive
- `features/`: boxscore, asof, ratings, massey, context, elo
- `model/`: score_model, dispersion, blend, calibrate, artifacts (JSON/NPZ plus metadata: git SHA, data hashes, library versions)
- `market/`: consensus, closing, market_ratings
- `eval/`: walkforward, metrics, bootstrap, `prereg.yaml`
- `products/`: kaggle, bracket_mc, pool, futures, card, ledger, clv
- `cli.py`: `doctor | ingest | log-odds | rate | predict | backtest | kaggle | bracket | pool | card | settle`

### W2 — As-of features
- Rewrite `features.py:240-608`; keep the per-game row builders at `:112-236`.
- **Grain:** team × as-of day. Every row uses only games strictly before its tip.

- **Massey** (men's only, optional input):

- **Context:**

- **Removed:**

  - `CurrTourney*`
  - `Diff_SeedRegionCode`
  - auto-included columns, replaced by an explicit whitelist

### W3 — Score model
- **Ratings engine, per gender:** weighted sparse ridge on points per possession.
  - `ppp = μ + O_team + D_opp + h·venue`
  - log-additive tempo: `poss = τ + T_A + T_B`
  - `E[pts_A] = poss·(μ+O_A+D_B±h)/100` gives margin, total and team totals consistently.
  - Re-solved every DayNum by updating the normal equations incrementally, which takes minutes in total.
- **Recency:** a T-Rank-style schedule (full weight for 40 days, sliding to 60% at 80+ days) against exponential half-lives of 30, 60 and 90 days, chosen walk-forward.
- **Prior:** last season's rating, regressed toward the conference mean (ρ 0.5–0.7). Worth 3–8 games, fading out by February.
- **Home court:** home-court advantage estimated per season with shrinkage (it has drifted down and collapsed in 2021); Huber or soft-capped margins for blowouts.
- **Context effects:** a ~10-parameter ridge GLM on the residuals of as-of predictions (should-have).
- **XGBoost residual model:** a challenger only, with a hard stop on Jan 31; the first thing cut.
- **Dispersion:**
  - `σ_M = a·√(poss/68)`, optionally plus a three-point-rate term; same form for σ_T. Fit on held-out data by Gaussian likelihood.
  - The correlation between the two teams' scores is ρ_AB = (σ_T²−σ_M²)/(σ_T²+σ_M²), about 0.29 in the 2025 market residuals.
  - Overtime branch: about 5% of games are tied in regulation, and OT adds roughly 15 to the total. Simulate integer scores so pushes price correctly.
  - Normal vs Student-t tails chosen by log score, with a PIT tail check before trusting alternate lines.
- **Calibration:** win probability = P(margin > 0) from the blended distribution.
  - Then Platt scaling, 2 parameters per gender, fit out-of-fold.
  - Blend first, calibrate last, no manual nudges, no isotonic.
- **Women's:** σ, home-court advantage and calibration fit separately. First- and second-round games on a top-16 seed's campus are home games for that seed.

### W4 — Market layer
- **Consensus from actual book prices.** Add power and Shin de-vig next to the tested math in `markets.py:72-145`.
- **Market-implied ratings:** a ridge fit of closing spreads and totals over the last 30–45 days. They price any matchup: bracket simulations, every Kaggle pair, futures.
- **Prediction-mode blend:** model vs market-implied, weight fit walk-forward (expect the model's share to be 0.3 or less).
  - **Kaggle:** Round-of-64 pairs use real near-close lines; later rounds use market ratings.
- **Betting test:** regress (actual − close) on (model − close). A slope near 0 means no edge.

- **Walk-forward 2012–24** (skipping 2020).

- **Metrics:**

### W6 — Products
- **`bracket_mc.py`:** the bracket structure comes from data (Kaggle 2027 slots or a manual CSV; reuse `bracket.py:25-104`); 76 teams.
  - At least 100k simulations: rating uncertainty drawn once per simulation, game noise per game, total variance matched to the calibrated σ.
  - Completed games locked; women's host venues.
  - Outputs advancement odds per round and fair futures.
- **`kaggle.py`:**
  - Driven by the sample-submission IDs; men's and women's, all pairs.
  - Women's home-court advantage applied per pair if they would meet in a hosted pod.
  - Validator: row count, P(i,j)+P(j,i)=1, [0,1] range.
  - Validated on 2022–25 and a 2026 replay. Submit early, resubmit late.
- **`pool.py`:** takes pool size, scoring and public pick %; returns the bracket that maximizes P(finishing 1st) by simulation.
  - Fallback: chalk plus a contrarian champion.
  - Pick-rate fallback: estimate from seed and AP rank.
- **`card.py` + `ledger.py` + `clv.py`:**
  - Fair prices at each book's own line for moneyline, spread and total. Team totals, alternate lines and futures are added once the PIT checks pass.
  - Best price among your books.
  - A disagreement flag (model vs market more than 4 pts on margin or 6 on total) and `config/availability_adjustments.csv` for injuries.
  - Ledger graded by EventID plus side TeamID (reuse `ledger.py:76-160`), with bankroll state and CLV.
  - **Stakes appear only if G5 passes.**

## Gates (`eval/prereg.yaml`)

Development period: walk-forward 2012–24. Every comparison uses a paired bootstrap 95% confidence interval.

- **G1 — ratings:**
  - The time-travel test gives identical features.
  - On games after Jan 15, margin RMSE beats raw net rating by ≥0.5 and Elo by ≥0.15, and is within 0.8 of the closing line.
  - Per season, |margin bias| < 0.5 and |total bias| < 1.0; pooled NCAA-tournament |total bias| < 1.5.
  - 50% and 80% intervals cover within ±3 pp of nominal.
- **G2 — any added component** (GLM, XGBoost, Massey, travel):
  - It ships only if CRPS or Brier improves with the confidence interval clearing 0, by ≥0.05 RMSE or ≥0.0005 Brier.
  - And it must not hurt pooled tournament metrics by more than 1 SE.
- **G3 — win probabilities and Kaggle:**
  - Out-of-fold calibration slope 0.9–1.1, |intercept| < 0.05, ECE < 0.02.
  - Pooled tournament Brier ≥0.008 better (men) and ≥0.005 better (women) than the seed-only model, and better than the ridge floor (0.190).
  - Men's no worse than Massey consensus +0.002.
  - The blend ships only if it beats pure ratings by ≥0.002.
  - If the 2026 holdout lands outside its 90% band, revert to ratings plus Platt.
- **G4 — scores vs market:** the blend's margin and total RMSE is no more than the closing line's +0.05.
- **G5 — betting.** Up to 6 segments (men's/women's × spread/total/moneyline), Holm-corrected for multiple testing. All three must hold:
  - The regression slope from W4 has its confidence interval above 0.
  - Historical ROI at near-close best-book lines is ≥ +2% on 1,000+ bets, and positive in at least 3 of 5 seasons.
  - Forward CLV ≥ +1.0 pp of no-vig probability, with the confidence interval above 0, over 400+ bets.
  - **Pass:** quarter-Kelly, ≤1% per bet and ≤3% per day, one bet per game per market, automatic stop if the trailing-200-bet CLV goes negative.
  - **Fail:** fair lines only.
